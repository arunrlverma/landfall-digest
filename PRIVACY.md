# Landfall — Privacy Policy

**Landfall is made by NextBase LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801.**
**Effective July 2026 · Contact: verma.arun@gmail.com**

Landfall is built so that there is almost nothing to have a policy about.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Landfall has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no
tracking, and no server of ours that receives your data. We cannot see your
books, notes, listening history, or anything else.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your books, margin notes, bookmarks, listening positions, podcast
subscriptions, downloaded audio, and settings are stored on your device (and
in your own iCloud device backup, under Apple's terms). Spoken notes are
transcribed **on-device** using Apple's speech recognition.

## What leaves your iPhone, and when

- **OpenAI — only if you add your own API key, and only when you use an AI
  feature.** Starting a Talk conversation streams your voice and the current
  passage to OpenAI; converting a book's reading level sends its text;
  note-tidying sends the note; premium narration sends the sentence being
  read; podcast discussion may send a short audio excerpt for transcription.
  This happens directly between your device and OpenAI under
  [OpenAI's API terms](https://openai.com/policies/); we are not in the
  middle. Your key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and is removable in
  the app at any time.
- **Apple's podcast directory and each show's own RSS feed** are fetched
  directly to browse and stream episodes.
- **The daily digest** is a public, identical-for-everyone file fetched from
  GitHub. Reading it sends GitHub nothing but an ordinary web request.

## What we never do

No selling of data, no sharing with data brokers, no fingerprinting, no
third-party SDKs. The app contains no code from advertising or analytics
companies.

## Children

Landfall is not directed at children and is rated accordingly on the App
Store.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with
